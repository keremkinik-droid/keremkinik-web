I am pleased to share that our chapter, *"Humanitarian Diplomacy and the Turkish Red Crescent: An Exposé on Theory and Practice"* (co-authored with Hafize Zehra Kavak), has been published in **Understanding Humanitarian Diplomacy: Principles and Practice** by Routledge.

The chapter examines how a National Society operating at the crossroads of major humanitarian crises — from the Syrian refugee response to the Kahramanmaraş earthquakes — practices humanitarian diplomacy: negotiating access, mobilising partnerships, and advocating for the most vulnerable while safeguarding the Fundamental Principles.

You can access the chapter via [DOI: 10.4324/9781003503187-16](https://doi.org/10.4324/9781003503187-16).
